Hi Feruza,

Thank you for reaching out and for your interest in contributing to The
Guardian Nigeria. We are always open to well-crafted, relevant content that
adds value to our readership.

We do occasionally accept sponsored editorial contributions that meet our
editorial and compliance standards. Each submission is carefully reviewed
by our content desk to ensure it aligns with our publication’s tone,
quality benchmarks, and audience relevance.

Where a submission is accepted for publication, an administrative charge
applies to cover editorial review, editing, formatting, and integration on
our platform. This ensures that all accepted content is presented
professionally and remains permanently accessible on The Guardian Nigeria.

Please note that we do not accept or publish content related to casinos,
pornography, vaping, CBD, or narcotics in any form.

To proceed with a review, kindly share the following:

   1.

   Proposed headline and topic
   2.

   A brief summary or draft of the article
   3.

   The website or brand being referenced (if applicable)

Once received, our team will review and advise on next steps, including
confirmation of suitability and the applicable administrative fee.

We look forward to the opportunity of featuring your work on The Guardian
Nigeria, one of Africa’s most respected national news platforms.
